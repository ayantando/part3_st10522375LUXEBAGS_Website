About Us

Welcome to Luxe Bags . We are an online shopping website created to provide customers with quality products and a simple, convenient shopping experience while serving fashion .

The vision of Luxe Bags is to become a recognized and trusted fashion brand across more countries while continuing to inspire people to embrace their individuality and pursue their dreams. Luxe Bags aims to expand its international presence while spreading its message of “Be a Dreamer” and encouraging customers to express themselves through fashion.

Our Story

Luxe Bags was formally introduced in 2024 after beginning as an idea in a notebook in 2022. The organization was founded by Ayanda Msomi, a young entrepreneur from Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, with the vision of creating a brand that combines chic, luxury and vintage-inspired fashion using bags.Luxe Bags began gaining recognition on the 28th of July 2024 and gradually expanded its customer base across South Africa. As Luxe Bags grew, it extended its reach to neighboring countries, such as Namibia and Zimbabwe. In 2025, Luxe Bags entered a partnership with South African artist Sabelo Mzobe. This collaboration increased the brand's visibility and contributed to its growth beyond the South African market. Luxe Bags focuses on providing stylish and distinctive bags for both men and women. The brand combines modern fashion with luxury and vintage-inspired designs to appeal to customers who value individuality and quality.

The organization was built around the belief that ideas can become reality when people are willing to dream and pursue their goals. This philosophy is represented by the brand's motto: "Be a Dreamer."

Our Mission

The mission of Luxe Bags is to deliver fashionable, practical and accessible bags for both men and women. Luxe Bags is focused on providing stylish, high-quality, and affordable bags that allow customers to express their individuality. The organisation aims to create a comfortable and convenient online shopping experience while offering chic, luxury, and vintage-inspired designs.
